About Me

I grew up in Himachal Pradesh, India, surrounded by stunning landscapes, a close-knit community, and vibrant culture, yet with limited resources. These experiences instilled in me the value of education, the influence of environment on life’s trajectory, and the importance of curiosity and growth. Twelve years at boarding schools fostered independence, discipline, and adaptability, shaping how I approach challenges today.

After high school, I immigrated to the United States, adapting to American culture while blending it with my Indian heritage to develop a truly global and diverse perspective. I earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from the University of Washington's Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering. I now build my career in technology as a Staff Software Engineer at Tesla Inc., creating scalable, user-centric fintech solutions that advance Tesla’s mission to accelerate the world’s transition to sustainable energy.

I approach life with integrity, reflection, and intentionality, seeking to grow, make meaningful impact, and cultivate relationships grounded in respect and shared principles. Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, reading, cooking, and traveling, finding moments that inspire learning, gratitude, and creativity.
